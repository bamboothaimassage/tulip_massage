Our Menu

Treatments

Every treatment can be booked for 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes. Not sure what to choose? Just ask – we'll recommend the perfect massage for you.

01

Traditional Thai Massage

The original Thai healing massage. Performed fully clothed, your therapist uses palms, thumbs, elbows and gentle yoga-like stretches along the body's energy lines (Sen). It improves flexibility, eases stiffness and leaves you feeling light and re-energised.

Stretch · Balance · Energy
02

Thai Sports Deep Tissue

Firm, focused pressure that reaches the deeper layers of muscle. Ideal for gym-goers, runners, footballers and anyone with knots, tightness or long-standing aches.

Firm pressure · Recovery
03

Thai Hot Oil Massage

Thai massage techniques combined with warm oil for smooth, soothing strokes. A beautiful balance of relaxation and therapeutic pressure.

Warm · Soothing
04

Full Body Aromatherapy

Gentle, flowing massage with fragrant essential oils chosen to calm the mind, lift the mood and help you sleep better. Pure relaxation from head to toe.

Calm · De-stress
05

Neck, Back & Shoulder

Targeted relief for the areas that carry the most tension – perfect for desk workers, drivers, or anyone short on time who needs to unwind quickly.

Targeted · Quick relief
06

Full Body Hot Oil Massage

A complete, nourishing full-body massage with warm oil and long, gliding strokes. Relaxes every muscle and leaves your skin silky smooth.

Full body · Nourishing
07

4 Hands Massage

Two therapists, four hands, one perfectly synchronised massage. With twice the touch, your mind can't focus on any one place – so it simply lets go. An unforgettable treat, or a wonderful gift for someone special.

Two therapists · Ultimate luxury
